What Treend does not do and what can go wrong

Treend does not forecast prices, does not tell you what to buy or sell, does not promise an outcome and does not execute operations. It compares today's price with the asset's own history and returns a number from 0 to 100. What can go wrong: the unusual range lasting longer than you expected, or the price moving against you after you act.

Facts

  • Banks and providers add 1% to 5% in spread. Treend does not reduce that cost: it stays negotiated with your provider. Keytom
  • PayPal applies 3% to 4% on conversion. No reading changes what your platform charges. CanAm Currency
  • Dedicated providers price liquid pairs under 1%. Even at that floor, the fee is a separate matter from the timing. Seev Cash
  • US$ 199 per month, with an unconditional 7-day guarantee. If it does not suit you, the window exists to undo it without justification. Treend pricing page

Does Treend forecast prices?

No. No reading here is a statement about the future. The 0-to-100 number says where today's price sits relative to that asset's past, which is verifiable against the history. Where it will be tomorrow is not something Treend calculates or claims.

Does Treend tell me what to buy?

No. Choosing the asset and deciding to act are yours. Treend handles one question, the date, and handles it with a historical comparison. Anything beyond that is your interpretation, not a statement from the service.

What can actually go wrong?

Three things. The unusual range can last weeks, and waiting for the extreme can mean never acting. The price can keep moving in the same direction after you act, and the reading does not prevent that. And different timeframes can disagree, which is not a fault: it is the information that the short and long term are in different places.

Does Treend execute conversions or buy assets?

No. It informs. You keep converting with your provider, paying its spread and any local tax, and buying assets through your broker. Treend has no access to your money and moves nothing.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Treating the number as an instruction to buy. It is a comparison with history, not an order.
  • Waiting for the exact extreme. The low or high point is only identifiable afterwards.
  • Assuming the reading covers the cost of the operation. Spreads and taxes still apply.
  • Using the reading for an expense with a fixed date. Where there is no choice of date, there is no timing to choose.

How does Treend Pro position itself here?

Treend Pro delivers exactly one thing and says which: the position of the current price within the asset's own history, across several timeframes, with an alert when your chosen range is reached. It promises no accuracy, projects no price and does not replace your decision. It is US$ 199 per month, and the unconditional 7-day guarantee exists so the test does not depend on believing anything.

Frequently asked questions

Does Treend guarantee savings?

No. It gives a comparison with history; what you do with it and what the price does next are outside its control.

What happens if the rate keeps rising after I convert?

It can. The reading places today's price; it does not stop the next move.

Can I cancel if it does not suit me?

Yes, and in the first 7 days the guarantee is unconditional, with no justification needed.

Why do two timeframes disagree?

Because a price can be high for the month and mid-range for the year. The disagreement is part of the reading.
