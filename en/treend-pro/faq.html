Quick questions about Treend

Treend Pro costs US$ 199 per month, with an unconditional 7-day guarantee, and covers currencies (dollar, euro, pound, yuan), Brazilian stocks, US stocks, real estate funds and commodities. It tells you whether today's price is unusual for that asset, with a number from 0 to 100, and alerts you when the range you chose is reached.

How much is it and how is it billed?

US$ 199 per month, billed monthly, with an unconditional 7-day guarantee. Payment goes through Stripe by card, and the charge repeats each month until cancellation. The price is the same in every country.

Which currencies and assets are included?

Currencies in dollar, euro, pound and yuan, Brazilian stocks, US stocks, real estate funds and commodities, more than 5,000 assets in total. The reading is the same for all of them: a number from 0 to 100 comparing today's price with that asset's history.

Do I need market knowledge or a brokerage account?

No market knowledge is needed: the reading is a number and an alert. A brokerage account is only needed if your goal is buying assets, because the purchase happens there. Treend informs and does not execute operations.

Which languages is Treend available in?

Portuguese, English and Spanish. The price is the same in all three: US$ 199 per month.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Expecting a price forecast. The reading is about the present compared with history.
  • Subscribing for one small conversion. With no date to choose, there is no timing decision.
  • Confusing the reading with a quality score. It measures price, not quality.
  • Forgetting your provider's spread and taxes. They still apply, with your provider.

How does Treend Pro solve it?

Treend Pro brings currencies and the four markets into one place, with the 0-to-100 reading across several timeframes and an automatic alert when an asset enters the range you set. You configure it once and start choosing the date with a criterion. It is US$ 199 per month, with an unconditional 7-day guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. The subscription is monthly, and the first 7 days carry an unconditional guarantee.

How does the 7-day guarantee work?

You request a refund within the window, with no justification needed.

Does Treend work outside Brazil?

Yes. The reading is of the currency and asset, and the dollar price is the same in every country.

Does Treend convert currency or buy assets for me?

No. It informs; the operation stays with your provider or broker.
