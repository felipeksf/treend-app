Who Treend is for and what you need to know

Treend is for anyone with a date to choose involving money: being paid or paying in a foreign currency, converting a balance, travelling, or buying an asset already chosen. You do not need to understand financial markets or read charts: the reading arrives as a number from 0 to 100 and an alert.

Who does Treend work well for?

Freelancers and professionals paid in dollars or euros who choose when to convert. People paying suppliers abroad with time before settling. Travellers who can buy currency in advance. And investors in stocks, real estate funds or commodities who have already chosen the asset and want to choose the date.

Do I need to understand financial markets?

No. The reading arrives as a number from 0 to 100, saying whether today's price is unusual for that asset, and as an alert when the range you chose is reached. There is no chart to interpret and no technical vocabulary to learn first.

Who is Treend not for?

Anyone making a single small conversion on a date someone else set. If you need to exchange a low amount on a day that is not your choice, there is no timing decision to make, and the subscription does not pay for itself. Better said here than discovered later.

Do I need a brokerage account?

Not to use Treend: it informs, it does not execute. You keep converting with your own provider and buying assets through your own broker. If your goal is investing, the brokerage account is needed for the purchase, not for the reading.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Subscribing for one small conversion. With no date to choose, the cost is not justified.
  • Expecting Treend to pick the asset. It handles the date; the asset is your choice.
  • Assuming you must watch a screen daily. The alert exists so you do not.
  • Using one reading only. The comparison period has to match your decision.

How does Treend Pro serve these cases?

Treend Pro gives the same 0-to-100 reading for currencies and the four markets, across several timeframes, and alerts you when your chosen asset enters the range you set. The freelancer uses it to convert, the importer to settle, the investor to time a contribution. It is US$ 199 per month, with an unconditional 7-day guarantee to test whether your case is one of them.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work outside Brazil?

Yes. The reading is of the currency and the asset, not of the user's country.

Does it work for someone who only buys currency for travel?

It works if you can buy in advance. If the trip is next week, the date is not a choice.

Do I need to invest in stocks to benefit?

No. The currency use is independent of any investment.

How long until I am using it?

You choose the asset and the alert range in the first session; there is nothing to install or integrate.
